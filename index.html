About SOSroofings

We’re a family-owned roofing company built on trust, hard work, and a commitment to doing things the right way. For us, roofing isn’t just a service—it’s a responsibility to protect the homes and families in our community.

Over the years, we’ve learned that every roofing project is more than materials and labor. It’s about giving homeowners peace of mind. That’s why we take the time to walk you through your options, explain the process clearly, and make sure you feel confident every step of the way.

As a family business, we value honesty, reliability, and attention to detail. We treat every home as if it were our own, using quality materials and proven techniques to deliver results that last. Whether it’s a small repair or a full roof replacement, we approach each project with care and pride.

What sets us apart is the personal connection we build with our clients. When you work with us, you’re not dealing with a large, impersonal company—you’re working directly with a team that genuinely cares about your home and your experience. We’re proud to serve our community and to stand behind every roof we install.
